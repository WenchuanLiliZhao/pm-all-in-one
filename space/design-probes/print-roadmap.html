Paper & ink palette

The ground is warm, the ink is warm, and hue is spent exactly once. Everything the old theme said with colour, this one says with weight, rule, and texture.

Task · V1StGXR8_Z5jdHi6B-myT · In progress · 29 Jun – 11 Sep 2026

Four theme blocks became one. The previous palette maintained light, dark, auto-light and auto-dark variants of every semantic token, which meant that any new colour was a four-way decision and that the app carried two visual identities at once. A printed object has a single ground. We keep one, warm it, and stop pretending that a sheet of paper has a night mode.

The ink is not black and the paper is not white. Text sits at #191710, a warm near-black that reads as pigment rather than as screen; the ground is #f7f3ea, with two darker bands reserved for ruler and plate areas. Every division that used to be a one-pixel grey box is now a hairline rule at low alpha, and shadow.scss is gone rather than re-valued — a page has no z-axis, so elevation was never describing anything real.

That leaves the question this task exists to answer: with --color-use--danger, --warn, --success and --accent all retired, how does a reader tell five statuses apart at a glance?

“Issues are Markdown documents you own on disk. The aesthetic should argue the thesis, not decorate it.” Handoff — editorial direction

What replaces the four hues

Duration bars carry status as a printing treatment. An outline is a shape that has been drawn but not yet inked; a solid black bar is work set in final ink; a hatched and struck bar is a plate pulled from the run. Only one status gets pigment, and it is the one that is happening now.

Open questions

  • Contrast on a warm ground needs auditing at the two lightest ink steps; --ink-30 currently fails against --paper-band at small sizes.
  • The terminal panel stays a dark plate pinned to its own palette. A dark rectangle on a light page is native to print, so this is not an exception to the law — but the seam where its border meets paper still needs drawing.
  • Spot colour is doing three jobs at once: selection, the today rule, and in-progress. If that reads as noise at full workspace scale, the today rule should drop to ink and keep hue for status alone.
